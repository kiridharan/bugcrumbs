# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in Bugcrumbs to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through their GitHub profile at https://github.com/kiridharan. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
